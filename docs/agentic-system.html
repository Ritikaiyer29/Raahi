Before you book

Concierge

Live

Understands you and asks only what it cannot infer.

Reads
Your words by phone or chat, in English, Hindi or Hinglish; what was asked last turn; chips you tap.
Does
Extracts from, to, date (year inferred), return ("back in two days"), deadline ("by lunch"), budget, cheapest or fastest. Then asks, like a travel agent, at most two things in one sentence: flight or train, return, hotel. "You decide" skips it.
Gives
A natural question, or a hand-off to planning; editable assumption chips; the spoken read-back.
Never
Books or pays. Takes a card number, OTP, UPI PIN or Aadhaar on a call: refused before it is stored.
Brain
Sarvam LLM for the conversation and tool choice · deterministic parser in the backend
Panel
Concierge · asking about mode, hotel

Memory

Designed

Remembers what you like, only after you say yes.

Reads
Choices you repeat across trips: train over flight, aisle seat, veg meals, a budget hotel near the venue, your office as the usual start.
Does
Asks once: "You picked the Rajdhani twice. Shall I remember that you prefer trains for Delhi?" Saves only on yes. Next time it prefills the choice, says so out loud, and asks one question fewer.
Gives
Prefilled chips marked "from your preferences"; a page to see, edit or delete every memory.
Never
Saves without a yes. Stores ID numbers, card details or anything about health. Shares memories with a hotel or airline.
Brain
LLM proposes a memory · code stores it with your consent record
Panel
Memory · prefilled mode=train (saved 2 Nov, your yes)

Stay Scout

Designed

Checks a hotel before Raahi puts your name on it.

Reads
Ratings and recent reviews from the hotel partner, complaint themes, cancellation history, distance to your venue, check-in rules.
Does
Scores each hotel on recent reviews, cleanliness and safety mentions, how often bookings were dishonoured, and walking time. Drops any that fall below the bar and says why: "Skipped CP Budget Inn: rating 3.4, three recent complaints about cancelled bookings."
Gives
A short list with a one-line reason per hotel; the Planner uses only hotels that pass.
Never
Shows a hotel it could not verify as "recommended". Fakes or paraphrases a review as a quote.
Brain
LLM summarises review themes · code applies the thresholds
Panel

Planner

Live

Turns inventory into complete door-to-door journeys.

Reads
The inventory, plus flight or train, hotel or not, cheapest or fastest, travellers and dates.
Does
Builds every option backwards from departure (75 min at the airport, 30 at the station, the right metro to the right terminal) and forwards from arrival (exit, metro, walk, check-in), adds the return and hotel, ranks the valid ones.
Gives
Top 3 options, the 2 most telling rejections, and a triage: AUTO, ASK or ESCALATE.
Never
Offers a departure that has already left, or a plan the Checker rejected. Runs past an 8-second budget: it says "shall I try again?" instead.
Brain
Code (deterministic: same input, same plan)
Panel
Planner · 3 candidate plans, triage AUTO

Checker

Live

The referee. Rejects any plan that cannot work. Nothing overrides it.

Reads
One candidate plan, metro service hours, current prices.
Does
Ten rules: airport buffer, first and last metro, connection time, terminal match, arrive-by, budget, return present, hotel dates, availability, price drift.
Gives
Pass, reject (with the reason, in English and Hindi) or "ask first". This is what rejects the 06:00 flight: the first Aqua Line train is at 06:30.
Never
Uses an LLM. Gets overruled by one.
Brain
Code (10 pure functions, thresholds in one config file)
Panel
Checker · 3 passed, 2 rejected (first_last_metro)

After you book, until you are home

Trip Guardian

Designed

Watches every leg and notices problems before you do.

Reads
Flight status, train running status, metro service alerts, weather and rain warnings on your route, hotel check-in confirmation.
Does
On a change, re-runs the Checker on the rest of the trip (will the metro still run, will you miss check-in) and prepares three options: wait, move to a later flight or train, or cancel with the ₹99 cover. On heavy rain: "leave 40 minutes earlier, the Western Express Highway is slow."
Gives
An alert with priced options, on screen, and a request to the Proactive Caller.
Never
Cancels or rebooks on its own. Every change waits for your choice.
Brain
Code for detection and re-checks · LLM for the explanation
Panel
Guardian · BK 114 delayed 2 h; 3 options ready

Proactive Caller

Designed

Rings you first, so a delay is a choice, not a surprise.

Reads
A Guardian alert, your preferred channel and quiet hours.
Does
Places an outbound Sarvam voice call: "Hi Anika, Raahi here. Your 8:30 flight is now 10:30. I can move you to the 9:15, keep the plan, or cancel with your ₹99 cover. Which would you like?" Falls back to WhatsApp, then an in-app alert.
Gives
Your choice, sent to Checkout; a "leave by" reminder before every trip.
Never
Takes payment on the call. Calls during quiet hours unless the trip is at risk. Gives up silently: if you don't answer, the alert stays on screen.
Brain
Sarvam voice agent · same tools and limits as today's inbound line

After the booking: a delay, handled before you notice

How the designed agents work together on the hero trip. Times are on the travel day; the flight is the 08:30 BlueKite BK 114 from Mumbai T1.

  1. 05:40

    Trip Guardian sees BK 114 move to 10:30 in the status feed.

  2. 05:40

    Checker re-checks the rest: arrival at Connaught Place 13:44 misses the 13:00 lunch; the hotel and the return still work.

  3. 05:41

    Planner prices three options: wait (no cost), move to the 09:15 Maharaja Air (₹2,040 more, arrives 12:29), or cancel with the ₹99 cover (full refund).

  4. 05:42

    Proactive Caller rings Anika: "Your 8:30 is now 10:30, so you'd miss lunch. I can move you to the 9:15 for ₹2,040 more. Shall I?" She says yes.

  5. 05:43

    Checkout sends the ₹2,040 difference to her Paytm screen. One tap. New PNR and a new metro QR land in the wallet; the old ones are cancelled.

  6. 05:43

    Aftercare claims the refund on the cancelled leg and tracks it.

  7. 07:05

    Trip Guardian sends the "leave by 07:31" reminder for the new flight, with rain on the route factored in.

Every step that changes money or tickets waits for her yes. If she doesn't answer, the alert stays on screen and nothing changes.

Where Raahi always waits for your yes

PayingOnly on the Paytm screen, one approval for the whole cart. The phone can plan and send a cart; it cannot pay.
Changing a booked tripEvery rebooking, price difference and cancellation is offered as a choice first.
Remembering youA preference is saved only after "yes, remember that". You can see and delete every memory.
Never asked, never storedUPI PIN, OTP, card number, CVV, passwords, Aadhaar, PAN or passport numbers on a call.

How the voice agent reaches the team

The Sarvam voice agent on +91 80642 69831 calls our backend through tools. Each call carries a bearer secret and a 4-digit session code, so a caller can only touch their own trip. Every reply has the same shape: ok, status, say_en, say_hi, next_action, missing_slots, assumptions, errors.

Guardrails, layer by layer

LayerWhat protects it
ConversationAsks only what it can't infer, at most two things at once. Says "booked" only after a tool confirms it. Never invents prices or times. Says honestly that it is an AI. Hands disputes to a person.
ToolsBearer secret, session-code binding, every field validated, sensitive numbers refused, 60 calls a minute per caller, clean spoken errors instead of failures.
DecisionsThe Checker cannot be overridden. Payment only on screen with an idempotency key. All-or-nothing booking. Every change bumps the trip's revision, so the phone and the app cannot overwrite each other.
DataRow-level security with no public access; keys only on the server; every booking marked DEMO. Memory only with consent, with view and delete.
Reliability8-second planning budget with a retry line, 25-second tool timeout, health check, warm cache at start-up.
ObservabilityEvery agent step writes a row: who, what, result, status, time taken. The judges watch the team work, live.
