Only 12% of travellers trust AI to decide alone. So Raahi does the work autonomously but asks for a yes at every moment that matters: paying, changing a booked trip, remembering preferences. Card numbers and OTPs are refused on the call; our tool layer refuses them before they reach the database, and we showed this live in testing. Every plan is triaged: auto, ask, or escalate to a person.
