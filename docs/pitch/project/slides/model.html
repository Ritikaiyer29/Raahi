Three ways Raahi pays for itself

More per trip

Hotels earn rivals 12 to 18%, flights 7 to 8%. A plan with a stay and add-ons earns more.

Measure: items per trip

New travellers

People who only pay with Paytm are one sentence from a trip; commuting brings them back daily.

Measure: first-time travel bookers

A licensed agent

Paytm already sells AI agents to banks and insurers as Pi. A trip agent fits that catalogue.

Measure: licence plus per-trip fee
